# Steve Jobs sample sheet

`steve-jobs.json` is a `life-grid/1` file built from Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Jobs). Import it from the menu ("Import a sheet").

- **Confirmed months** (14): dates the article gives with a month.
- **To confirm** (17, `confirmed: false`): events the article dates only by year, or whose month comes from general knowledge rather than the article.
  Year-only items sit in a month of that year chosen just to keep them apart: the year is real, the month is a placeholder.
  They show as "to confirm" and do not count toward the percentage.
- No filler: every one of the 31 cells is an event. Homes are kept for the colors.
- From general knowledge, not the article text I read: the Apple IPO, iMac, iPod, iPad, Toy Story, the transplant, his resignation as CEO and the Palo Alto home (from about 1994).
- Two cells carry event silhouettes in the grid: Jan 1984 (Macintosh) and Jun 2007 (iPhone).
- The file has a `death` entry (5 Oct 2011), so the grid stops there.
