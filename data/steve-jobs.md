# Steve Jobs sample sheet

`steve-jobs.json` is a `life-grid/1` file researched in November 2026 from Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Jobs) and the sources below. Import it from the menu ("Import a sheet"). The source of every cell is in `research/steve-jobs.sources.json`; the raw research files are `research/steve-jobs-*.json`.

- **Confirmed months** (164, 24.1% of 681 months): a source gives the month. 49 of them are "In the news" cells, where the month comes from a dated article or listing.
- **To confirm** (14, `confirmed: false`): the year is sourced but the month is a placeholder chosen to keep items apart. They show as "to confirm" and do not count toward the percentage.
- No filler: every one of the 178 cells is an event.
- A month is only filled when a source gives that month. Where several things happened, the cell holds the most notable one.
- Dates come from Apple and Pixar releases, Time, Fortune and Washington Post archives (some only as search snippets), Stanford, and Wikipedia citations. NYT, LA Times and Wired could not be opened.
- Homes: adds Los Gatos from Jan 1983 (Time, 3 Jan 1983); stay: India Jun-Dec 1974 (approximate). The other move months (Mountain View, Los Altos, Cupertino, Woodside, Palo Alto) are still year-only.
- Still unverified: 1955-75 has almost no month-level evidence; 1992 has no cell; the April 2009 transplant month may be March.
- The file has a `death` entry, so the grid stops there.
- Search was rate-limited during research, and several sites (NYT, BBC, Guardian) could not be opened; dates taken from search snippets or Wikipedia citations are listed in the research notes.
