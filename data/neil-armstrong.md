# Neil Armstrong sample sheet

`neil-armstrong.json` is a `life-grid/1` file researched in November 2026 from Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Armstrong) and the sources below. Import it from the menu ("Import a sheet"). The source of every cell is in `research/neil-armstrong.sources.json`; the raw research files are `research/neil-armstrong-*.json`.

- **Confirmed months** (188, 19.1% of 985 months): a source gives the month. 13 of them are "In the news" cells, where the month comes from a dated article or listing.
- **To confirm** (13, `confirmed: false`): the year is sourced but the month is a placeholder chosen to keep items apart. They show as "to confirm" and do not count toward the percentage.
- No filler: every one of the 201 cells is an event.
- A month is only filled when a source gives that month. Where several things happened, the cell holds the most notable one.
- Dates come from NASA history volumes and mission pages, Purdue pages, Wikimedia Commons file pages quoting NASA, US Navy and DVIDS captions, the X-15 flight record, and Wikipedia citations of Hansen's "First Man".
- Homes: adds Purdue (1952), Cleveland (1955), Lebanon, Ohio (1971, was Cincinnati) and Indian Hill (about 1994); stays: Hawaii and Korea (USS Essex) in 1951-52.
- Still unverified: childhood and Eagle Scout months, the 1944 move, the Edwards years 1953-61, the X-20 selection, the NAHF month; 1987, 1990, 1992-93 and 1998 have no cell.
- The file has a `death` entry, so the grid stops there.
- Search was rate-limited during research, and several sites (NYT, BBC, Guardian) could not be opened; dates taken from search snippets or Wikipedia citations are listed in the research notes.
