# Neil Armstrong sample sheet

`neil-armstrong.json` is a `life-grid/1` file built from Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Armstrong). Import it from the menu ("Import a sheet").

- **Confirmed months** (35): dates the article gives with a month.
- **To confirm** (8, `confirmed: false`): events the article dates only by year, or whose month comes from general knowledge rather than the article.
  Year-only items sit in a month of that year chosen just to keep them apart: the year is real, the month is a placeholder.
  They show as "to confirm" and do not count toward the percentage.
- No filler: every one of the 43 cells is an event. Homes are kept for the colors.
- Not in the article text I read: the Challenger commission year (1986), the Apollo 13 month and the NASA move to Houston (Sep 1962) come from general knowledge.
- The file has a `death` entry (25 Aug 2012), so the grid stops there.
