# Tim Berners-Lee sample sheet

`tim-berners-lee.json` is a `life-grid/1` file researched in November 2026 from Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Berners-Lee) and the sources below. Import it from the menu ("Import a sheet"). The source of every cell is in `research/tim-berners-lee.sources.json`; the raw research files are `research/tim-berners-lee-*.json`.

- **Confirmed months** (168, 19.6% of 858 months): a source gives the month. 14 of them are "In the news" cells, where the month comes from a dated article or listing.
- **To confirm** (10, `confirmed: false`): the year is sourced but the month is a placeholder chosen to keep items apart. They show as "to confirm" and do not count toward the percentage.
- No filler: every one of the 178 cells is an event.
- A month is only filled when a source gives that month. Where several things happened, the cell holds the most notable one.
- Dates come from his own MIT CV and W3C pages (including the official history), CERN and INSPIRE records, Crossref and the official book site (2026 tour dates). BBC, Guardian and NYT could not be opened.
- Homes: the 1980 Geneva contract is a stay, Boston starts Oct 1994 (W3C founded; the actual move month is unverified), and he owned a house in Cessy, France around 1990. Later Oxford and London homes are not verified.
- Still unverified: 1955-75 (childhood and Oxford months), the Emanuel School entry year (1966 or 1969 by source), Image Computer Systems dates; 2023 has no cell.
- Search was rate-limited during research, and several sites (NYT, BBC, Guardian) could not be opened; dates taken from search snippets or Wikipedia citations are listed in the research notes.
