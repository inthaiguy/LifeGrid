# Tim Berners-Lee sample sheet

`tim-berners-lee.json` is a `life-grid/1` file built from Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Berners-Lee). Import it from the menu ("Import a sheet").

- **Confirmed months** (18): dates the article gives with a month.
- **To confirm** (17, `confirmed: false`): events the article dates only by year, or whose month comes from general knowledge rather than the article.
  Year-only items sit in a month of that year chosen just to keep them apart: the year is real, the month is a placeholder.
  They show as "to confirm" and do not count toward the percentage.
- No filler: every one of the 35 cells is an event. Homes are kept for the colors.
- He is living as of the article, so the grid runs to the current month.
- The Poole, Ferndown and Boston moves use the year or the start of the job the article names; the exact month is approximate.
