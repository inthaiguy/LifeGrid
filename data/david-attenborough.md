# David Attenborough sample sheet

`david-attenborough.json` is a `life-grid/1` file built from Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Attenborough). Import it from the menu ("Import a sheet").

- **Confirmed months** (3): dates the article gives with a month.
- **To confirm** (23, `confirmed: false`): events the article dates only by year, or whose month comes from general knowledge rather than the article.
  Year-only items sit in a month of that year chosen just to keep them apart: the year is real, the month is a placeholder.
  They show as "to confirm" and do not count toward the percentage.
- No filler: every one of the 26 cells is an event. Homes are kept for the colors.
- He is living as of the article, so the grid runs to the current month. At 100 he is past the 88-year color ramp.
- Leicester from 1932 and the program months (Life on Earth, Blue Planet, Planet Earth, his 1973 BBC exit) come from general knowledge.
