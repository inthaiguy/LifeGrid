# David Attenborough sample sheet

`david-attenborough.json` is a `life-grid/1` file researched in November 2026 from Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Attenborough) and the sources below. Import it from the menu ("Import a sheet"). The source of every cell is in `research/david-attenborough.sources.json`; the raw research files are `research/david-attenborough-*.json`.

- **Confirmed months** (330, 27.3% of 1207 months): a source gives the month. 47 of them are "In the news" cells, where the month comes from a dated article or listing.
- **To confirm** (24, `confirmed: false`): the year is sourced but the month is a placeholder chosen to keep items apart. They show as "to confirm" and do not count toward the percentage.
- No filler: every one of the 354 cells is an event.
- A month is only filled when a source gives that month. Where several things happened, the cell holds the most notable one.
- Programme dates come from Wikipedia series pages, archived BBC pages, TheTVDB and public Listener issues; about 100 cells in the 1980s-2010s are dated programme listings (Wildlife on One, Wildlife Specials, Natural World). BBC Genome, the Guardian and the Times could not be opened.
- Homes: London is now Richmond, Surrey (Wikipedia, citing a 2014 local paper). He appears to be alive on 16 Nov 2026 (Wikipedia shows no death date; no independent news found).
- Still unverified: 1927-51 and 1962-64 are mostly empty; his marriage month, Clare College entry, National Service dates and the honour months (Gazette scans) are year-level only.
- Search was rate-limited during research, and several sites (NYT, BBC, Guardian) could not be opened; dates taken from search snippets or Wikipedia citations are listed in the research notes.
